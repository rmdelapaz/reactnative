Showcase

Five minutes of your app, two of questions.

Showcase · a slot

Five minutes, four parts

0–1The client, the people, no signal, out of scope
1–3On a phone: a must-have, offline or a permission, an empty state
3–4One decision in the code: a type, the must-never-break test
4–5The checks, large text or a screen reader, the time log
5–7Questions
Showcase · peers

Two stars and a wish

  • Two things that work well, and why
  • One wish: specific, kind, with the screen or the step
  • Signed, handed to the presenter
  • Feedback, not a score
Showcase · questions

The kind of questions you will hear

  • Which value do you compute instead of storing?
  • What does it do with no signal? Is anything sent twice?
  • Which rule does only your mock API keep? Its test?
  • What happens after "Don't allow"?
  • Where were your estimates furthest from the hours?
