6.4 · Mental model

The tide table on the van's dashboard

  • Copied before the season; read at dawn with no signal

  • A local reminder is the card; the app copies it again often
  • A push is the harbor radio: a station, a radio, a license
6.4 · Local or push?

Who knows it, and when?

LocalThe app decides ahead; the phone's scheduler shows it, even closed
PushA server decides when something happens; APNs or FCM deliver it
SignalLocal: not needed · Push: needed at that moment
Expo GoLocal works · Push: not on Android since SDK 53
WHY A reminder before a cleanup is known at sign-up: local.
6.4 · Asking to notify

The same gate as location

iPhoneAsks once; alerts and sounds only (no badge)
Android 13+Asks once a channel exists; once more after a no
Android ≤ 12On unless turned off in Settings
Web previewThe browser's question; no scheduler at all
PHONE From the platforms' and Expo's docs: your phone has the last word.

Everything around the sending

6.5

Haptics, Sharing and Files

Feedback you can feel, used with restraint; files that leave the phone.

6.5 · Mental model

The tap on the shoulder

  • No shouting "well done" across the beach for every bag
  • When it matters, a quick tap: you look up
  • It does not replace the words; it makes you look at them
  • Tap everyone every minute, and you are ignored
6.5 · Five rules

Sparingly, and never alone

  1. Outcomes, not presses
  2. The same meaning every time
  3. Never the only feedback: words on the screen
  4. Less is more: two moments in the whole app
  5. Optional: a switch, and the phone's settings

6.5 · Share it

The platform first, then the sheet

shoreline-crew/src/storage/logbookExport.ts
  if (Platform.OS === 'web' || !(await Sharing.isAvailableAsync())) {
    return 'unavailable';
  }
  const file = await writeLogbookFile(entries);
  await Sharing.shareAsync(file.uri, {
    mimeType: 'text/csv', // Android: which apps to offer; iPhone: the file's type
    UTI: 'public.comma-separated-values-text', // the same type, in Apple's words
    dialogTitle: 'Share your logbook', // the title of Android's share sheet
  });
  return 'done';
WHY 'done' = the sheet closed. The phone never says whether anything was shared.
