# Session 26 labs: Final Project Showcase and Final Assessment

Session 26 teaches no new lesson: you show your final project and take the written final assessment. Its one lab
is a tool for your project.

| Folder | What it is | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| `showcase-preflight/` | `evidence_check.mjs`: reads your final project's files and lists the evidence the Lesson 10.1 rubric asks for that is still missing. Run it two days before the showcase | Node 24; your final project's folder (nothing is installed, sent or changed) |

From your course folder (`shoreline`): `node labs/module-26/showcase-preflight/evidence_check.mjs ~/projects/your-app`
(the folder where your final project's `package.json` is). The last line says READY FOR THE SHOWCASE, READY WITH
NOTES or NOT READY. The check is a list of missing evidence, never a score: the rubric scores, with a person.
